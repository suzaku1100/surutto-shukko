Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy for "Slide Out — Car Escape Puzzle" (the "App"), published in Japan as「スルッと出庫」. 日本語版はこちら.

Our approach

The App does not require an account. The developer does not collect or store your personal information on our own systems. To show ads in the free version and to manage purchase status, the App relies on the following third-party services.

  • No account registration
  • No developer-run analytics platform
  • Your game progress is never sent to the developer's servers

Advertising

The free version shows ads (banner, full-screen, and video) served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Ad data such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • App activity such as launches and content views
  • Coarse location estimated from your IP address
  • Crash and performance information

For details on how Google handles data, see Google's policies and terms.

Before using the advertising identifier, the App shows the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog and waits for your response before starting ad delivery. Every feature of the App remains fully available even if you do not allow tracking. You can change this permission anytime in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you use the App from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent form is shown on first launch. You can change your choices anytime from the App's Settings → "Ad privacy settings".

If you purchase the one-time "Premium", ads are no longer shown.

Data stored on your device and in iCloud

Your game progress (clears, best records, and settings) is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud account (key-value storage). The developer cannot access this data. Deleting the App and its iCloud data removes this information completely.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Premium) are processed by Apple's App Store. The developer never receives your credit card details.

Purchase status is managed with services provided by RevenueCat, Inc. An anonymous user identifier, purchase history, and basic device information are sent to RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

Notifications

The Daily Puzzle reminder is delivered as a local notification on your device, and only if you turn it on in Settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the support page.
