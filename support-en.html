Frequently asked questions

Q. My Premium purchase isn't showing up

Try Settings → "Restore Purchase" in the App. You must be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.

Q. Will my progress carry over to a new device?

Yes — as long as you are signed in with the same Apple Account (iCloud), progress carries over automatically. Premium carries over with "Restore Purchase".

Q. Can I remove the ads?

Purchasing the one-time "Premium" removes banner and full-screen ads. You can buy it from Settings → "See Premium (one-time purchase)".

Q. I bought Premium but still see ads

Try Settings → "Restore Purchase". You must be signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with. If ads still appear after restoring, quit the App completely and launch it again.

Q. How do I stop personalized ads?

In the iOS Settings app, go to Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off tracking permission for this App. Every feature remains available either way. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you can also change your choices from the App's Settings → "Ad privacy settings".

Q. I can't solve a level

Every level is verified to be solvable. The "Hint" button shows the best next move (3 free hints per day; after that you can watch a video ad for one more, up to 5 per day — Premium gives unlimited hints without ads).
